I caught myself rehearsing a parent meeting in the shower — not the facts, the turns. If she says X, I'll say Y. Felt silly until I remembered Stokoe's book and that conversation often works like that anyway.

She studies real recordings: hostage talks, GP visits, dating agencies. A lot of the body-language advice we're sold doesn't hold up. Tiny word choices and pauses matter more. That matched my gut feeling and was oddly reassuring.

The bit about mediators steering people with phrasing nobody notices stuck with me. I still script difficult calls in my head. Now I worry less about the scripting and more about freezing when someone goes off-script. Practical book; made me feel slightly less odd.
