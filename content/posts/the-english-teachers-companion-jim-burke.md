Great book — I learnt a lot from it, especially in my first year, when everything felt improvised and slightly terrifying. (My notes called it a handbook; the copy on my shelf is Burke's *Companion*.)

There is no grand theory here, just answers to the questions that actually kept me awake: getting kids to read something hard, surviving the marking, reaching the kid who talks brilliantly but writes nothing. Rubrics, discussion moves, annotation — classroom stuff from someone who had done the job for years.

I still pull it out on Sunday nights when a lesson won't come together. Useful in the way a calm senior colleague is useful.
