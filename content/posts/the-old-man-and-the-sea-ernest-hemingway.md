I read this in one sitting on a train — Santiago, the marlin, the sharks, the skeleton mistaken for a shark at the end.

I enjoyed it, but the prose is so simple and repetitive that at points it feels like a drawn-out fable — yes, your hand hurts, we know, you said that two pages ago. At its best the stripped-back language has a chant-like rhythm; at its worst it outstays the fable.

The dream of the lions still landed. I liked it a lot without quite loving it — and the ending is what I remember.
